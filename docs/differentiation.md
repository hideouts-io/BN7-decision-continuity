# Independent product and differentiation hypothesis

Decision Continuity investigates one question: **Does our earlier decision still hold—and exactly what changed?** Its product direction and proposed UX are independent of a company portfolio. Repository, publication and tracking separation are operational work recorded elsewhere; this research does not establish that those external connections have been removed.

Primary sources reviewed October 6, 2026. The observations below come from public official documentation, illustrated workflows and product pages, without creating accounts or exercising commercial installations. They establish documented patterns, not exhaustive product capability, comparative performance, customer demand or uniqueness. Proposed behaviors and acceptance criteria are our design inferences. The README owns implemented behavior; TODO links the authoritative Linear milestones.

## Initial prospective customer and problem

Start with a technical-assurance reviewer reassessing an AI service or software integration after a declared configuration or permission change. AI governance and cybersecurity teams are prospective customers, not validated buyers. The first useful job is reconstructing the applicable decision, identifying the changed premise, preserving an unresolved question and recording an accountable outcome without losing the earlier basis.

Example: an integration was accepted for a read-only Production scope. A later capture declares write access with Unknown applicability. The reviewer defers pending clarification. A subsequent Production declaration opens a separately attributable replacement review. The original read-only boundary, frozen targets, deferral and later revision must remain inspectable. A permission declaration is not proof that an operation occurred.

The principal failure hypothesis is maintenance burden: a correct explanation may still cost more to capture and link than the reviewer saves. Visual novelty cannot resolve that uncertainty.

The visual implication is a task-oriented shell: a clear entry point, compact attention list, focused evidence/review workspace and explicit historical perspective. Use an original identity, generous hierarchy, restrained status color, readable record references and progressive disclosure. Rich dependency views must retain an equivalent text path. These are design proposals; passing browser checks proves behavior and basic accessibility, not that the navigation is intuitive.

Broader governance products already overlap materially. [IBM watsonx.governance](https://www.ibm.com/products/watsonx-governance) documents governance graphs, continuous monitoring and traceability across activities, decisions and model changes. [Vanta's TPRM guide](https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/11487926-third-party-risk-management-implementation-guide) describes side-by-side assessment comparison, review cadence and findings follow-up. Our narrower hypothesis concerns reconstructible decision bases and explicit frozen-target clarification handoffs. These sources do not establish that configurable enterprise products cannot support that workflow. The earlier IBM use-case documentation endpoint was unavailable on retrieval; this comparison uses its accessible official product page instead.

## Verified foundation and reconstruction boundary

HID-14 implements a read-only basis inspector for one v6 decision immediately after a selected recorded event. It derives a causal partial order from validated references, array append order and recorded times. Original creation and replacement/new-review pairs are atomic. Causally ordered equal-time events are inspectable; incomparable equal-time cuts, cycles and histories above 256 relevant events fail explicitly. Capture time does not establish recording order. Historical rule implementation versions are unavailable; the inspector reconstructs recorded facts rather than applying current rules to old evidence. See the README for the canonical contract and verification.md for actual results.

The differentiating combination under investigation is attributable source versions, independently scoped dependencies, explicit uncertainty, responsible clarification replacement and reconstructible frozen review context. Each component has precedents. No verified claim of uniqueness or groundbreaking performance follows from their combination.

## Cheapest useful evaluation

Use the existing small public/synthetic sequence: initial read-only decision → revised boundary → Unknown applicability → deferral → Production clarification → replacement → separately recorded revision. Add unchanged and ambiguous-order controls. The automated oracle checks reconstruction, references, invalid histories and preservation. It cannot substitute for a practitioner.

When a real participant is available, ask them to identify the earlier scope, exact change, unresolved question, responsible review and facts unavailable at the earlier cut. Compare the inspector with the ordinary record list, and later with two-cut comparison. Record correctness, assistance, time and input/link-maintenance burden only in an actual session; observations remain blank beforehand. The reviewer kit owns the session protocol and practitioner gate.

Continue investment if people reconstruct the basis correctly with less assistance/effort and describe a recurring workflow worth trialing. Simplify if record detail obscures the next action or duplicate capture overwhelms the benefit. Stop expanding infrastructure or change the target job if prospective users cannot identify repeat reassessment pain. Technical preparation and synthetic success are separate from comprehension, operational impact and demand; none of those customer outcomes has been validated.
